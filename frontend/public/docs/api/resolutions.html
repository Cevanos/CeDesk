Turn resolution receipts off, optional or required (Settings; audited)

PUT/api/v1/resolution-receipts

Off by default. With required, resolving a ticket without a receipt is refused (422), for the listed teams or every team. Automatic closes (auto-close, rules, Cevanos) never need one.

Request body (JSON)

FieldTypeDescription
mode requiredenumOne of: off, optional, required
required_teamslist of string (uuid)With required only: the teams whose tickets need a receipt. Empty: every team.

  -H "Authorization: Bearer $CEDESK_KEY" \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \

Resolutions: closed vs. with a receipt vs. verified (Supervisor, Admin)

GET/api/v1/reports/resolutions

Tickets resolved within [from, to) and still resolved (merged ones left out): how many have a receipt, how many were verified (by the agent, from Shopify or Stripe), have a pending item, or told the customer. Per team and per assignee.

Verify a receipt from the customer's Shopify order or Stripe payment

POST/api/v1/tickets/{ticket_id}/resolution/verify

Send one of order_id or payment_id (as the ticket's orders or payments card lists them). CeDesk reads it from the installed app, read-only, and sets result to what it reports (payment and fulfilment state, or refunded), verified to shopify or stripe, and verified_at. Nothing is written to the shop or Stripe. Same 7-day window as changing the receipt.

Parameters

NameInTypeDescription
ticket_id requiredpathstring (uuid)

Request body (JSON)

FieldTypeDescription
order_idstring or nullA Shopify order, from the orders card.
payment_idstring or nullA Stripe payment, from the payments card.
