Set the archive age (Settings; audited)

PUT/api/v1/archive/settings

Resolved tickets older than this move to the archive at the next nightly run. Retention still deletes them when it says so.

Find archived tickets by number or customer

GET/api/v1/archive/tickets

Resolved tickets older than the archive age (default 120 days) leave the ticket list and live here. Give number (1042 or T-1042), or the customer's email or contact_id; newest first, at most 50. A team-only role sees its team's.

Open an archived ticket (read only)

GET/api/v1/archive/tickets/{ticket_id}

Everything that was on it: messages (internal notes too, with an email's headers), files' names, rating, and in related: time logged, tasks, approvals, links, side conversations, Jira issues, emails sent and the Cevanos chat. The type's, team's and assignee's names are as they were when it was archived. A role that sees one team only gets a 404 for another team's. It can't be changed, replied to or reopened; a customer's late email answer to it becomes a new ticket that follows it.
