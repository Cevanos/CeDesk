At most 20 schedules per workspace and 10 recipients each. The worker works the report out under *your* rights each time and checks every recipient again, so someone who lost the right gets nothing; if you lose it, the schedule switches off.

Run a built-in report or a custom definition (Supervisor, Admin)

POST/api/v1/reports/run

A time series is zero-filled and bucketed in the workspace's time zone (weeks start on Monday). Imported tickets stay out of the time metrics, merged tickets out of everything, as in the other reports. A role limited to one team is held to it. A run longer than the server's limit is 422; too many at once is 429 with Retry-After.
