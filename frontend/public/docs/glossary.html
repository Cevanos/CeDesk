Glossary

The terms used across this help center, in plain words.

API key

A secret that starts with cdk_. It lets another system call CeDesk without a person signing in. Admins create and revoke keys.

Campaign

One email sent to the customers in a segment, with an unsubscribe link in every message.

Cevanos

A separate product for AI help and order information. CeDesk works without it. Once your workspace is connected to it, the AI buttons (summaries, rewrites, translations and drafts) appear.

CSAT

A short satisfaction survey sent to a customer after their ticket is resolved.

Contact

A person who writes to you or was imported. Each contact has a profile with their tickets, notes and custom fields.

Macro

A saved set of actions an agent applies to a ticket in one step.

Role

What a member may do. Built in: Admin (everything), Supervisor (adds assigning to others, approvals and reports), Agent, and Light agent (reads and adds notes only).

Segment

A saved filter of contacts. A campaign is sent to a segment.

SLA

The time targets for a first reply and for resolving a ticket. CeDesk warns you before and when one is missed.

Suppression list

The addresses campaigns never go to: people who unsubscribed or reported spam, bounced addresses, addresses added by hand, and erased contacts.

Ticket

One customer request and the conversation about it.

Webhook

A signed message CeDesk sends to your own web address when something changes on a ticket.

Workspace

Your organization's own separate space in CeDesk. You sign in with its address.
