Keep response times on track Draft

Last updated 2026-11-24 · Who: Supervisors and Admins

Overdue tickets build up quietly. A few steady habits keep them down.

Who can do this: Supervisors and Admins.

Watch the queue, not just the totals

Open the live queue at the start of the day. Look at tickets waiting for a first reply and at the ones due soon. Sort the ticket list by SLA to work the soonest-due first. See Find and sort tickets.

Keep targets honest

Set targets by priority that your team can meet, and put your real hours in. The clock pauses outside them and on holidays. See Response-time targets and Business hours and holidays.

Share the work evenly

Set a sensible capacity per channel, and ask agents to set themselves On break rather than leave tickets sitting. See Routing and agent availability and Your status and account menu.

Park what is waiting, and close what is dead

  • Use snooze for tickets that wait on something, so they leave your list and come back by themselves.
  • Turn on auto-close for Pending tickets the customer never answers.

Escalate early

Add a rule that raises the priority when the response-time clock runs out, and tell the team lead. See Rules.

Check it worked

The number of open tickets past a target in the team report goes down week by week.
