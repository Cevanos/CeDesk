Measure and improve Draft

Last updated 2026-11-24 · Who: Supervisors and Admins

Reports help only if someone acts on them. A short weekly routine is enough.

Who can do this: Supervisors and Admins.

Get the numbers to come to you

Set up a scheduled report with created and resolved per day, emailed to the team lead every Monday.

Ask the same four questions each week

  1. Is the pile growing? Read backlog history.
  2. Are we on time? Read the team report: first reply and SLA met.
  3. Are customers happy? Read CSAT and the comments on low ratings. See The satisfaction survey.
  4. What do people write about? Read tickets by topic and channel in More metrics.

Turn what you find into action

  • A topic that keeps coming back: write a help article, add a saved reply, or add a rule.
  • A high reopen rate: look at how tickets are resolved, and at auto-close.
  • One agent far busier than others: look at capacity and routing.

Keep a dashboard

Put your four or five key reports on one dashboard the team can open every morning.

Check it worked

The same report next week shows the change you made.
