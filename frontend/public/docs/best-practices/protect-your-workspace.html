Protect your workspace Draft

Last updated 2026-11-24 · Who: Admins

Support desks hold customer data, so protect it. Do these in order.

Who can do this: Admins.

1. Two-step sign-in for everyone

Turn it on for yourself first, then require it. See Security settings and Two-step sign-in. Keep recovery codes safe.

2. Single sign-on, if you have an identity provider

Single sign-on means people leave your company and lose access in one place. Sign in with it yourself before you turn on Only allow SSO.

3. Give the lowest role that works

Most people are Agents. Make a custom role for people who need only reports or only settings. See Roles and rights. Deactivate people who leave the same day, under Members.

4. One API key per system

Make a key for each system, with the lowest level that works, and revoke it when the system goes. See API keys.

5. Do not keep what you do not need

Set retention so old resolved tickets go. Turn on card number hiding under Topics and card redaction.

6. Watch the log

Read the audit log now and then, and turn on the email to Admins about security events.

Take care with the IP allowlist

It must include your own address, or you lock yourself out. Use Test my IP first.

Check it worked

Your security score goes up, and only the people you expect appear in the audit log.
