Account and access

Add people, roles and teams, sign in safely, set your status and manage API keys.

  1. Add and manage members — Invite people, give them a role, reset passwords and two-step sign-in, and deactivate leavers.
  2. Roles and rights — The built-in roles, and how to build a custom role with exactly the rights you want.
  3. Teams — Group agents so tickets are routed to the right people and shared out evenly.
  4. Sign in and passwords — Sign in, change your password and recover one you forgot.
  5. Two-step sign-in — Add a code from an authenticator app to your sign-in, and what to do if you lose your phone.
  6. Your status and account menu — Set yourself Available, On break, Transfers only or Offline, and find your account options.
  7. API keys — Let your own systems call CeDesk, choose how much access a key has, and revoke it.
