Automation

Rules, workflows, response-time targets, routing, topics and auto-close, so routine work runs by itself.

  1. How automation works — What happens to a ticket on its own, and the order it happens in.
  2. Rules — If a ticket matches these conditions, do these actions, such as route refunds to Billing.
  3. Test rules and workflows — Try a rule or workflow on a ticket, or on past tickets, without changing or sending anything, and read the run log.
  4. Workflows — Chains of steps that can wait, branch and follow up on their own, with versions you can restore.
  5. Response-time targets (SLA) — Set how fast tickets must get a first reply and be resolved, by priority, with policies for special cases.
  6. Business hours and holidays — Tell the SLA clock when your team works, and which days it stops.
  7. Routing and agent availability — How CeDesk picks an agent for a ticket, and how to set status, capacity and skills.
  8. Topics and card redaction — Give tickets a topic from keywords, and hide card numbers customers send.
  9. Auto-close — Resolve Pending tickets by themselves when the customer stops replying.
