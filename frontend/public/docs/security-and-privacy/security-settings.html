Security settings Draft

Last updated 2026-11-24 · Who: Admins

These settings decide how people sign in and what protects your workspace. They apply at once when you save.

Who can do this: Admins.

The security score

Go to Settings › Security & data › Security. At the top is a Security score out of 100, with a checklist: Single sign-on, Two-factor sign-in for everyone, IP allowlist, Strong password rules, Sensitive data hidden, Short sign-in sessions, Files need sign-in, Email Admins about security events and Customer portal limited to your domains. It updates when you save.

The settings

SettingWhat it does
Minimum password lengthFrom 8 to 64 characters.
Password expiresNever, or after 90 or 180 days. An expired password must be changed at the next sign-in.
Sign people out afterAn hour without use, or 8 hours, 12 hours or 7 days. A shorter setting also ends longer sessions already open.
Require two-factor sign-inEveryone must use a second step. People without it can only set it up until they do.
Files need sign-inAttachments open only for signed-in people, not for anyone holding the link.
Email Admins about security eventsAdmins get an email when a member is added, deactivated or reactivated, the IP list or the two-factor rule changes, or a member's two-factor is reset.
IP allowlistOnly these addresses can sign in.

Select Save.

Before you turn on two-factor

Turn it on for yourself first. If someone loses their second step, an Admin can reset it under Settings › Workspace › Members with Reset 2FA.

The IP allowlist

Type one address or range per line, up to 100. Empty allows everyone. The list is checked at sign-in and on every request. It must include your own address, or you lock yourself out. Select Test my IP to see whether your address is on the list before you save.

Check it worked

The score goes up after saving, and the "Last changed by" line shows your name.

Troubleshooting

What you seeWhyWhat to do
Someone is told their password expiredThe expiry setting is on.They choose a new password at sign-in.
Someone can only set up two-factorThe requirement is on and they have none.They finish setup.
Everyone is refusedThe IP list does not include their address.Fix the list from an allowed address, or ask whoever runs your server.
