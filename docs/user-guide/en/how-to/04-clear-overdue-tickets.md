> **Who can do this:** Supervisors and Admins for the live queue. Agents can sort the list.

1. Open **Reports › Live** and read **PAST SLA** and the list **Waiting for a first reply**.
2. On **Tickets**, sort by SLA so the most overdue are first.
3. Reply to or resolve the oldest ones. Snooze those that wait on something outside your control.
4. Select several tickets and assign the unassigned ones to an available agent in one go. You can change up to 100 at a time.
5. Turn on **Auto-close** under **Settings › Service** for Pending tickets nobody answers.

**Check it worked:** **PAST SLA** on the live queue falls.
