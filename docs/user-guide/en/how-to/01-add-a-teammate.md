> **Who can do this:** Admins.

1. Go to **Settings › Workspace › Members** and select **Add member**.
2. Type their name and email, choose a role, and type a temporary password. Select **Add member**.
3. Share the password with them privately.
4. Go to **Settings › Workspace › Teams**, select **Edit** on their team and tick them.
5. Ask them to sign in, change their password, and set their status to **Available**.

**Check it worked:** they sign in and appear on the **Agents** page as Available.

More: [Add and manage members](../account-and-access/add-and-manage-members.md), [Teams](../account-and-access/teams.md), [Roles and rights](../account-and-access/roles-and-rights.md).
