> **Who can do this:** Admins. CeDesk is not legal advice: email only people you may email.

1. On **Contacts**, filter to the customers you want and select **Save as segment**.
2. Go to **Campaigns**, select **Settings** and add your postal address.
3. Under **Settings › Channels › Email › Deliverability**, check that forwarding and DNS are fine.
4. Select **New campaign**, choose the segment and the sender, and write the subject and body.
5. Select **Send test** and read it on your phone.
6. Select **Send**, now or at a later time, and confirm.

**Check it worked:** the campaign changes to Sending, then Sent.

More: [What email campaigns are](../campaigns/what-email-campaigns-are.md), [Segments](../customers/segments.md), [Send campaigns responsibly](../best-practices/send-campaigns-responsibly.md).
