CeDesk is a help desk. Customers write to you by email, chat or your web portal. Every message lands in one queue where your team answers it, and CeDesk helps with the routine work around it.

> **Who can do this:** Everyone who has an account reads this. Nothing here needs a setting.

## The main ideas

| Idea | What it is |
|------|-----------|
| Workspace | Your company's own CeDesk. It has its own address to sign in with, its own people, customers and settings. |
| Ticket | One conversation with a customer about one problem. Every message, note and task about it stays on the ticket. |
| Contact | The customer. One profile shows all their tickets, notes and details. |
| Channel | How the customer reaches you: email, chat or the web portal. A ticket remembers which one it came from. |
| Member | A person on your team with an account. |
| Role | What a member may do. See below. |

The glossary explains any other word.

## Who can do what

| Role | What they can do |
|------|-----------------|
| Admin | Everything, including members, settings and campaigns. |
| Supervisor | What an Agent does, plus assigning tickets to others, approvals and reports. |
| Agent | Works tickets: replies, notes, tasks and time. |
| Light agent | Reads tickets and adds notes. Does not reply to customers. |

Light agents are free: they do not count towards your plan's limit on agents. Your Admin can also build custom roles.

## Where things are

The menu has **Tickets**, **Contacts**, **Agents**, **Archive**, **Campaigns**, **Reports** and **Settings**. After you sign in you land on **Tickets**. What you see depends on your role: for example, only Admins see **Campaigns**.

## What CeDesk does for you

- It can sort and route tickets by rules, and warns you before a response time (SLA) is missed.
- It keeps a history of everything done to a ticket.
- If your workspace is connected to Cevanos, AI buttons appear to summarise, rewrite and translate.

## Check it worked

Sign in. You land on **Tickets** and see the menu above, less any item your role does not allow.

## Troubleshooting

| What you see | Why | What to do |
|--------------|-----|------------|
| A menu item you expect is missing | Your role does not allow it. | Ask an Admin to check your role. |
| There are no tickets | The workspace is new. | See [Try CeDesk with demo data](try-cedesk-with-demo-data.md). |
