This is the order that gets a new workspace working with the least effort. Each step names the part of the menu to use. Later topics in this guide explain each one in full.

> **Who can do this:** Admins.

## The checklist

| Step | Where | Why |
|------|-------|-----|
| 1. Create the workspace | Sign-up page | You become the first Admin. See [Create your workspace](create-your-workspace.md). |
| 2. Try it with sample tickets | Settings › Security & data › Demo data | Learn the screens without real customers. Only where demo mode is on. See [Try CeDesk with demo data](try-cedesk-with-demo-data.md). |
| 3. Invite your team | Settings › Workspace › Members | Choose **Add member** and pick a role for each person. New members start Offline until they set themselves Available. |
| 4. Connect support email | Settings › Channels › Email | So customer email arrives as tickets. |
| 5. Set response-time targets | Settings › Service › SLA | CeDesk warns you before one is missed. |
| 6. Check your plan | Settings › Security & data › Plan & retention | Know your limits. See [Your plan and usage](your-plan-and-usage.md). |
| 7. Tidy up the demo tickets | Tickets | If you loaded demo data, resolve or delete those tickets before real customers write in. They are marked **Demo**. |

## Check it worked

A test email to your support address appears under **Tickets**, and your team can sign in and see it.

## Troubleshooting

| What you see | Why | What to do |
|--------------|-----|------------|
| A teammate cannot sign in | They have not been added, or are deactivated. | Check **Settings › Workspace › Members**. |
| Test email does not arrive | The email address is not connected or verified. | Open **Settings › Channels › Email** and run its checks. |
