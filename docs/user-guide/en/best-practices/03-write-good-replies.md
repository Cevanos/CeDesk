Fast replies are good only if they still read like a person wrote them.

> **Who can do this:** Everyone who replies. Admins build saved replies and macros.

## Use saved replies for facts

Keep the facts that never change, such as refund timing and shipping times, in [saved replies](../working-with-tickets/saved-replies.md). Insert one with `/`, then add a line that is about this customer. Use the customer's first name through the placeholders.

## Use macros for routines

A refund that always needs a reply, a tag and a resolve is a [macro](../working-with-tickets/macros.md). One click, and nothing is half done, because if a step is refused none are saved.

## Leave private notes

Write [private notes](../working-with-tickets/reply-and-add-private-notes.md) for what the next agent needs to know: what you tried, who you asked and what you are waiting for. Mention a colleague with @ so they are told.

## Use AI as a draft, not an answer

If Cevanos is connected, [suggest reply and improve](../working-with-tickets/ai-help-with-replies.md) give you a start. Read it, correct it and send it yourself. Nothing is sent without you.

## Keep it short and clear

- Answer the question in the first line.
- Say what happens next and when.
- Add a help article link if one exists.

## Check it worked

Your replies take less time to write, and customers reply with thanks, not with the same question again.

## Troubleshooting

| What you see | Why | What to do |
|--------------|-----|------------|
| A placeholder shows as a dash | The value is empty for this customer. | Edit the text before sending. |
| Replies feel stiff | Too much of the text is canned. | Add a personal first line. |
