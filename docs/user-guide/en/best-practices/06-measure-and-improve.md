Reports help only if someone acts on them. A short weekly routine is enough.

> **Who can do this:** Supervisors and Admins.

## Get the numbers to come to you

Set up a [scheduled report](../reports/scheduled-reports.md) with created and resolved per day, emailed to the team lead every Monday.

## Ask the same four questions each week

1. **Is the pile growing?** Read [backlog history](../reports/backlog-history.md).
2. **Are we on time?** Read the [team report](../reports/team-report.md): first reply and SLA met.
3. **Are customers happy?** Read CSAT and the comments on low ratings. See [The satisfaction survey](../channels/satisfaction-survey.md).
4. **What do people write about?** Read tickets by topic and channel in [More metrics](../reports/more-metrics.md).

## Turn what you find into action

- A topic that keeps coming back: write a help article, add a saved reply, or add a rule.
- A high reopen rate: look at how tickets are resolved, and at auto-close.
- One agent far busier than others: look at capacity and routing.

## Keep a dashboard

Put your four or five key reports on one [dashboard](../reports/dashboards.md) the team can open every morning.

## Check it worked

The same report next week shows the change you made.

## Troubleshooting

| What you see | Why | What to do |
|--------------|-----|------------|
| Numbers look odd | Merged and imported tickets are left out of some numbers. | See [Reports overview](../reports/reports-overview.md). |
| Few ratings | Survey is off, or few customers answer. | Turn on the survey, and read all comments. |
