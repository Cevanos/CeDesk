Your status tells CeDesk whether to give you new tickets.

> **Who can do this:** Everyone who takes tickets. Light agents have no status.

## Set your status

Open the account menu in the top corner. Under **My status**, choose:

| Status | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| Available | You can be given new tickets. |
| On break | Away for a while. You are not given tickets. |
| Transfers only | You take only tickets handed to you by hand. |
| Offline | You are not working. |

New members start Offline. Remember to set yourself Available when you start work.

## The rest of the menu

The menu also has **Theme**, **My account** (change password and two-step sign-in), **Change password** and **Sign out**. **My devices** on the account page, for phones that get push notifications, is marked as coming soon.

## Check it worked

Your status shows on the **Agents** page within 30 seconds.

## Troubleshooting

| What you see | Why | What to do |
|--------------|-----|------------|
| You get no new tickets | Your status is not Available, or you are at capacity. | Set Available, and check capacity with your Admin. |
